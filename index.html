Research

Neural circuit reorganization and recovery after injury

2026
Wormery Lab, University of Leeds

Studied how neural circuits reorganize and recover after injury using C. elegans as a model system.

  • Built and validated a full computational pipeline: linear oscillators, FitzHugh-Nagumo single-neuron modeling, coupled and chained neuron networks, and a lesion-and-recovery protocol.
  • Implemented oscillator simulation infrastructure in Python, including semi-implicit Euler integration, empirical measurement functions, convergence checking, and timestep selection.
  • Investigated node loss by silencing individual oscillators and explored compensatory coupling from surviving neighbors as a recovery mechanism.

Conversational agent for older adults

Jul. 2025 – Current
Interactive Computing Lab (Prof. Uichin Lee), KAIST

Designed and developed a prototype Q&A conversational agent using RAG and LLMs to help older adults navigate smartphone apps, starting with YouTube, plus a context-aware overlay that surfaces help directly within the app.

Co-authored a paper on this work, submitted to IMWUT (PACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies) as fourth author. Received a decision of major revisions with a strong chance of acceptance pending the revised submission.

Induced pluripotent stem cells for personalized drug discovery

Feb. 2024 – Jun. 2024
Brain Repair Laboratory (Prof. Minee Choi), KAIST
