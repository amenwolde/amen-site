ClothCall

2026
Addis Ababa, Ethiopia / Daejeon, South Korea

A native Android app for totally blind adults in Ethiopia that detects clothing stains and fading through a phone-call interaction metaphor, built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

  • Designed a discreet phone-call interaction model and a caregiver-calibrated fading threshold, where a trusted person sets a reference photo and tolerance level.
  • Ran a formative field study with five participants recruited through HPDO in Addis Ababa, interviewed in Amharic, which narrowed the app's scope to stain and fading detection.
  • Co-authoring a CHI Extended Abstracts paper on the project's design contributions, including "right to know first" and "feedback etiquette" as a design domain.
